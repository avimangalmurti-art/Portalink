Privacy Policy

Version: portalink-public-2026-10-16-v1

1. Operator and scope

Avinaash Mangalmurti personally operates Portalink. Contact avi@portalinknetworks.com about privacy or a request. This notice describes the proposed launch configuration; its review banner means it is not a certification of current live settings. Acknowledging a privacy notice records that you received it. It is not consent to advertising, analytics, promotion, or AI uses.

New registrations under this launch are limited to people aged 18 or older in the United States. We do not offer a child or teen registration flow. If you believe an ineligible account or a child’s information has been submitted, contact us so it can be assessed without sending additional identity documents or sensitive records.

2. Information we process

Account, profile, and work

We process account identifiers, the sign-in email and verification state, credentials handled by our authentication service, profile name and handle, optional profile/contact/location details you enter, preferences, and the work and uploads you submit. Projects can include text, images, documents, audio, video, descriptions, covers, citations, dates, and associated file metadata. Original files may contain location or other personal information. Automatic stripping of all such metadata is not established for this launch.

Your sign-in email is used for account operation rather than intentionally displayed as a public profile contact. An address or other detail you deliberately add to a public profile or publish within your work is public. Check the fields and audience you choose.

Eligibility and agreement records

The proposed eligibility check uses the country and full birth date you enter to assess the US/18+ requirement. The date is used transiently by the application and, for Google sign-in or eligible account completion, sent in the screening request. It is not written to the launch receipt or Google handoff tables. The receipt records the account, US designation, adult age band, agreement versions, and server time. This is self-declaration rather than independent age or location verification. Not storing a date in those tables does not establish that infrastructure logs omit request bodies; those protections remain a launch verification requirement.

Google sign-in, when enabled and chosen

Google authenticates you and provides basic identity information such as your name, email address, profile image, verified-email state, and stable Google account identifier. The requested scopes are openid, email, and profile. This flow does not request Gmail messages, Drive files, contacts, or your Google password. Google also processes your interaction under its own privacy practices.

Interactions and communications

We process follows, likes, saves, reposts, comments, citations, notification state, messages, attachments, conversation settings, and reports needed to provide the actions you choose. Public comments identify their author. A save explicitly described as named can disclose the saving account to the creator. Messages are available to their participants; they are not public posts. The operator and service providers may access information as needed for service operation, security, support, reports, and lawful requests.

Browser and infrastructure information

Cookies and browser storage support sign-in sessions, security, preferences, drafts, and cached content. Google sign-in also uses temporary state, nonce, and PKCE values to protect the exchange. Providers may process IP addresses, device/browser information, request times, and operational or security logs. The full logging inventory, access rules, processing locations, and backup retention remain under verification for this draft. A private account does not mean the operator or necessary providers can never access its data.

3. Purposes and optional uses

We use information to authenticate accounts, assess eligibility, record agreement versions, deliver and organize work for its chosen audience, provide messaging and named interactions, manage notifications, maintain reliability and security, investigate reports, respond to requests, and comply with legal obligations.

New optional reading-activity and performance collection is disabled for this proposed version. Previously collected records may remain until a reviewed retention or request process addresses them. Disabling new collection does not delete existing records. Essential account, security, message, and deliberately chosen interaction processing continues.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or send marketing email under this version. Essential verification, recovery, security, request, and service messages are distinct from marketing. We do not use your personal information or submitted content to train AI models, or grant optional AI retrieval or reproduction rights through signup. Optional AI access is unavailable. A future optional feature requires a separate review and specific permission where required, rather than treating this notice or the Terms as consent.

4. Where information is held and who receives it

Portalink uses the existing Supabase project for authentication, account and application records, Realtime features, and file Storage. The Sites worker also holds page interactions, related named events and notifications, and historical reading/performance information in a separate D1 database. This D1 information is not stored in Supabase. A request must consider both stores.

Service providers include Supabase; OpenAI Sites and its hosting infrastructure, including Cloudflare D1; Google for chosen sign-in and Google Workspace for the public contact inbox; and the configured authentication-email delivery service. Providers process information needed to deliver those services. We are still verifying the relevant provider processing locations, operational logs, access, contractual terms, and backup rules. This draft does not promise that all processing occurs in one region or that every provider copy can be erased immediately.

Other people receive work and profile details you make public, messages you send them, and the identity or activity disclosed by the action you choose. Public material may be indexed, cached, copied, or redistributed outside our control. We may disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to address a security risk, investigate abuse, or protect legal rights. We consider the request’s authority and scope and protect unrelated information.

5. Retention and removal

We retain account information and work as needed to provide the account, its chosen features and audience, and to handle requests, security, disputes, and legal obligations. We review retained information for those purposes rather than treating it as a permanent archive. When no longer needed, information should be removed or de-identified through a verified process. This draft sets no universal deletion or backup-expiry deadline.

  • Browser eligibility staging expires after up to 30 minutes. A Google handoff has a 10-minute logical expiry. These limits prevent reuse of the protocol records; physical cleanup depends on the separately verified cleanup process and can be delayed by failures.
  • Agreement receipts retain a minimal account/version/eligibility record for demonstrating the applicable agreement and handling legitimate security or legal needs. Abandoned signup receipts require their own retention review.
  • Messages, attachments, replies, and shared records can involve other participants. A request is assessed to remove or de-identify your information where appropriate while protecting their rights. Closing your account does not automatically erase their copies.
  • Historical D1 events, snapshots, reading summaries, and operational logs need separate treatment. The actual D1 erasure procedure is unresolved; a Supabase-only deletion cannot be represented as complete.
  • The deletion design under review would keep permanent security records in D1 so delayed requests cannot recreate removed information. They contain only immutable internal account, project, and deletion-job identifiers, a digest identifying the deletion plan, and its recorded time—not content, handles, or email addresses. These identifiers remain a retained security record, not a promise of complete erasure. This design is not activated; its exact scope and retention require final policy review before public activation.
  • Backups, provider logs, and necessary security or legal-hold records may have different retention. The relevant schedules and restore safeguards are not yet verified. We will explain known limitations affecting a request and will not describe an unverified copy as erased.

Changing visibility limits future access through the service but cannot recall material already received by others. Existing signed media links or locally cached content may remain usable for their applicable lifetime. Sign-out is not a request to delete the account or every local copy.

6. Access, correction, export, and deletion requests

Email avi@portalinknetworks.com with the type of request and the relevant account or public profile link. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, tokens, identity documents, or a full private archive in the first message. You do not need to create an account just to make a request. An authorized agent can contact us with evidence of authority; we may verify with the account holder where appropriate.

Before disclosing private information or performing a sensitive action, we will ask for the minimum information reasonably needed to verify ownership and the request. Existing account access and verified account-email control can be used; a matching display name or public handle alone is insufficient. Verification information is used for that purpose and not for marketing or unrelated profiling. If verification is not possible, we will explain the limitation and available next steps without disclosing another person’s data.

You may ask for access, correction, a portable copy, deletion, or a review of how your information is used. Applicable law may provide additional rights, exceptions, and response deadlines. We will assess those requirements, respond within applicable deadlines, explain an extension or limitation where allowed, and avoid retaliation for exercising a right. This draft does not assert that every privacy statute applies to Portalink.

In-app account deletion remains disabled. Manual requests require review across Supabase, Storage, Sites D1, and relevant provider records. Complete erasure across these systems has not been established; we will explain what has been completed, retained, or remains unresolved. Public Portfolio PDF and bibliography exports are convenient limited outputs, not a complete personal-data export. A public Portfolio PDF excludes private/hidden work, source archives, and conversations. A broader request is reviewed individually. A protected delivery method must be agreed and verified before private information is sent; a complete account-archive and delivery process has not yet been demonstrated for this draft.

7. Browser choices and security

You can use browser controls for cookies, storage, and cached files, and the service’s available account and visibility controls. Disabling essential storage can prevent sign-in or account features from working. Portalink does not currently vary its behavior in response to the legacy Do Not Track signal; the optional reading/performance collection and advertising uses described above are disabled for this version. We do not claim a separate browser-signal detection feature that has not been implemented.

We use access controls and session protections intended to reduce unauthorized access, but no service can guarantee absolute security. Previously delivered work and valid cached or signed links cannot always be recalled instantly. Report a suspected exposure to the contact above with a short description and only necessary evidence.

8. Changes and contact

A material change will have a new version and notice before it applies. A required new consent or content permission will be requested separately; an old acknowledgment will not be treated as permission for a new optional use.

Privacy requests and questions: avi@portalinknetworks.com. The proposed Terms of Service describe content permissions, reports, restrictions, and appeals.
