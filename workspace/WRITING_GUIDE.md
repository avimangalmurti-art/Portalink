# Portalink writing guide

Portalink speaks in a calm, direct, creator-focused voice. Copy should help people understand what is happening and what to do next without sounding promotional, playful during errors, or more technical than the task requires.

## Vocabulary

- **Creator** — a person with a public profile.
- **Profile** — a creator’s public identity and categories.
- **Category** — a named group of projects on a profile.
- **Project** — one published body of work.
- **Work** — projects collectively, especially in discovery.
- **File** — an image, video, audio file, PDF, or document attached to a project.
- **Home** — the published-work feed.
- **Portalink** — location-based discovery through the atlas and globe.
- **Messages** — conversations between people.
- **Inbox** — notifications such as replies and follows.
- **Studio** — analytics and research about published work.

Avoid using *item*, *content*, *portal*, *store*, or *app* when one of these terms is more precise.

## Patterns

- Use sentence case for headings, buttons, alerts, and settings.
- Label actions with verbs: **Create category**, **Preview project**, **Save changes**.
- Keep errors neutral and useful: state what failed, preserve entered work, and provide the next action.
- Use **Try again** only when the same request can succeed on a retry. Use a more specific label such as **Retry upload** when the recovery is narrower.
- Empty states explain what belongs there and, when possible, name the action that fills the state.
- Use *my* and *your* only when ownership prevents ambiguity. Do not use *we* in interface copy.
- Field labels state what to enter; placeholders show a format or short example.
- Accessibility labels describe the result of an action, not the appearance of its icon.
